## Featured Project

### Attack, Defence and League Points in the 2025/26 Premier League

**Research question:** How are team attacking and defensive
indicators associated with league points?

This project analyses match records and player statistics.
The workflow includes checking data quality, cleaning data,
aggregating team statistics and visualizing relationships.

**Key findings:** Goals scored per match have a strong positive
association with league points, while goals conceded per match
have a strong negative association. Both attacking and defensive
performance are closely associated with points in this dataset.

**Limitation:** The team-level analysis covers only 20 teams
in one season. Goals also directly determine match results and
points, so these associations do not establish causation or
predict future performance.
